Record a payment (check, card, Zelle, …)

Method = how you paid (check / credit card / Zelle / custom). Category = what it is for the books: Operating · Loan (P&I) · Owner draw · Tax · Asset · Client reimbursable (parts for a job you will get paid back for — not OpEx).

Reimbursable: company card buys parts for a client → record here as Client reimbursable + method Credit card. When they Zelle you back, use the Money in tab as Reimbursement (optionally link this payment). That is not sales income — it clears the amount due from the client.

Principal reduces loan balance (not P&L). Interest hits Interest Expense on the P&L.

Party B / Party A product remittances

Use this when product sales belong to someone else and the cash still lands in the company bank. Not owner equity draws — these are proceeds owed. Set the fee rate in the EasyCart settings if you import orders. Party labels are whatever you type in Settings.

1 · Accrue amount owed (monthly)

After the month’s sales: enter Party B (or Party A) product sales, fees, and the amount you owe them. Or enter the remittance amount directly. This increases Due to Party B / Due to Party A and records remittance expense (not a draw).

2 · Record payment / retention

Party B: bank wire/ACH — also available under Payments out → category “Pay Party B remittance”.
Party A: if you keep the cash in the company account, record the method Retained in company bank (clears the amount owed without a transfer).
